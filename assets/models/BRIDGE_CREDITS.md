# Reference photographs for the footbridges

The timber footbridges over the brook (`tools/blender/build_bridge.py`) were modelled from these openly licensed photographs: how the boards are laid, their width and gaps, the stringers and sills, the spacing of posts and rails, the braces, and the colour of weathered oak. Nothing from them is copied into a texture.
