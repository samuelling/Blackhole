# Garden of Delight: the story so far

The background narrative for the game, as Sam set it out (26 Nov 2026). Everything playable sits inside it.

## The fleet

- Earth became uninhabitable centuries ago, after **The Event**.
- A fleet of probes was sent out to find where life can continue. Each one is hurtling towards a final destination where it can establish a recolonisation.
- **We are one of those probes.** The ship is the bubble: the garden, the field and the land they stand on.

## What the land carries

- **Earth's blueprint** is embedded in the land.
- So is a **static snapshot of the entire human population in 2027**, taken before The Event.

## What went wrong

- A few centuries into the journey, **our ship lost its navigation**.
- **The wormhole** was built to connect the ships of the fleet. It's the one out in the field, where things come through.
- One of the other ships carries the **Shred Hole**. It has a fabricator, but **no Earth blueprint any more**: a Shred Hole accident compromised it. What it shreds comes to us through the wormhole.
- Before it went offline, another ship sent word that it had found something it called **World Builder**. We have only partial coordinates for it.
- The Shredder has established a **vortex portal** to World Builder. We are still working out where it is, and how to get there.

## The crew's task

It is up to the crew to **re-establish our navigation**, using the Shred Hole and the garden.

## Meanwhile

We pass the time:

- gardening
- building the dry stack wall
- building a gate, or whatever else suits our fancy

## Told and playable now

- **The intro film** tells this story the first time you enter (`src/film.js`; made with Fable), in Brodie's voice. Half of it is shot in the game itself: our ship is the real bubble, the wormhole the real one. *The story so far*, under the title, plays it again.
- **Star navigation**, the first step to getting our nav back (`src/nav.js`). The garden's sky is the real one, with true star positions. The Shred Hole fabricates a **bubble sextant**; it comes through the wormhole. You climb a tree above the haze, take the heights of three stars, and work the fix the way navigators did: each star gives a line the ship must be on, and where they cross is where we are.

## Still to come

- The next steps of the navigation: knowing where we are is not yet knowing where we're going. The partial coordinates for World Builder are waiting.
- Eventually, the Shred Hole housed in a **building on a planet you can travel to**.
