# Betamax's guns, in the garden

The guns (src/betamax/) come from Betamax (samuelling/betamax): procedural models at true scale, and two of them are dressed in
downloaded models, which are here:

**Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0), from Sketchfab**, resized and recompressed, otherwise unmodified:
- "low-poly SIG MCX Spear" by D_U — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-sig-mcx-spear-36ac159795ae465e8a4d44068bbaf434 (`models/spear.json`, the M7)
- "low-poly VHS 2" by D_U — https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-vhs-2-e70118269cc548d09de332088fc88904 (`models/bullpup.json`, the X95)

The gunshots are synthesized (src/betamax/gunsound.js), fitted in Betamax to recordings of real rifles.
