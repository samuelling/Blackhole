# Reference photographs for the ironwork

The entrance gates, their overthrow and the rose arches (`tools/blender/build_ironwork.py`) were modelled from these openly licensed photographs: how the leaves are framed, the dog bars, scroll bands, swan-neck top rails and spear heads, the scrollwork overthrow, and the rose arches' ladder sides and fanned heads. Nothing from them is copied into a texture.
