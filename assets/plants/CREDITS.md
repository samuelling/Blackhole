# Reference photographs for the plant models

The plants in this folder were modelled in Blender by `tools/blender/build_plants.py`. Their shapes, counts, proportions and colours were read
from the photographs below, all from iNaturalist and all released CC0 (public domain) or CC BY (attribution). No photograph is copied into a
texture: the leaves and petals are drawn and painted by the script. The photographers are credited here all the same, with thanks.
